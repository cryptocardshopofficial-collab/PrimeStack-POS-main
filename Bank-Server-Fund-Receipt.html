Bank Server Fund Receipt
How to receive real transaction funds from external bank servers into the PrimeStack platform — ISO 8583 host-to-host, SWIFT, ACH, Wire
ℹ
PrimeStack supports four methods for receiving real funds from bank servers. Each method creates an immutable double-entry ledger entry crediting your platform reserve. The method you use depends on the sending bank's available connection type.
01 — Connection Methods
🏦
ISO 8583
Host-to-host real-time. Bank connects directly to PrimeStack ISO 8583 gateway on port 8583.
🌐
SWIFT / Wire
International wire via SWIFT BIC. T+1 settlement. Funds arrive to your registered Wise/bank account.
⚡
ACH / FedWire
US domestic ACH same-day or FedWire real-time. Routing + account number required.
🔄
SEPA
EU SEPA credit transfer. IBAN + BIC. T+1 settlement across 36 SEPA countries.
📡
T2T (Terminal)
Terminal-to-terminal direct transfer. External terminal calls POST /t2t/transfer on your PrimeStack URL.
🔒
S2S (Server)
Server-to-server encrypted transfer. External PrimeStack node sends via authenticated S2S connection.

05 — Recording Receipt in Dashboard
1
Log in to Admin Dashboard
Go to http://localhost:9006/login and sign in with admin credentials.
2
Navigate to Receive Funds
In the left sidebar go to Platform → Receive Funds. Select the tab matching your method: Bank Server (ISO 8583), Bank Account, or T2T.
3
Enter Transaction Details
Enter the amount, currency, sender bank name, source account (masked), bank transaction reference/ID, and select destination platform account.
4
Submit Receipt
Click Receive from Bank Server. The platform posts an immutable double-entry ledger entry and logs the audit event.
5
Verify Balance
Go to Ledger → Accounts and check PLATFORM:RESERVE:USD balance has increased by the received amount.
