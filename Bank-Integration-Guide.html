Receiving Bank Integration
Required information for registering your bank as a creditor destination on the PrimeStack payment network
ℹ
Once your bank is registered, PrimeStack automatically populates ISO 20022 creditor fields in every outgoing payment instruction. You will not need to manually enter bank details for each transaction.
01 — Required Bank Information
FieldDescriptionFormatStatus
Bank Name Full legal name of the financial institution Text, max 140 chars REQUIRED
Account Holder Name Exact name on the bank account (must match bank records) Text, max 140 chars REQUIRED
SWIFT / BIC Code Bank Identifier Code for international transfers AAAABBCCDD (8 or 11 chars) REQUIRED
IBAN International Bank Account Number (SEPA / international) GB64TRWI60846482676861 REQUIRED*
Account Number Domestic account number (US ACH / local accounts) Numeric, max 34 chars REQUIRED*
Routing Number ABA routing number (US wire and ACH only) 9-digit numeric REQUIRED (USD)
Currency Primary settlement currency of the account ISO 4217 — USD, EUR, GBP, AED REQUIRED
Country Country where the bank account is domiciled ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 — US, GB, DE REQUIRED
Bank Address Full registered address of the bank branch Street, City, Postcode, Country OPTIONAL
Sort Code UK sort code for GBP domestic payments 60-84-64 (6 digits) GBP ONLY
Intermediary Bank Correspondent bank SWIFT/BIC if required CHASUS33 OPTIONAL
Connection Type Payment rail used for settlement SWIFT / SEPA / FEDWIRE / ACH / LOCAL REQUIRED
* Either IBAN or Account Number is required depending on the payment corridor. For US domestic accounts provide Account Number + Routing Number. For SEPA/international provide IBAN.
02 — Integration Steps
1
Complete the Bank Details Form
Fill in all required fields from Section 01 above. Ensure account holder name matches exactly as it appears on your bank statement.
2
Submit to PrimeStack Operations
Send the completed form to your PrimeStack account manager or via the secure admin portal at Platform → Bank Connections → Register Bank.
3
Verification & Approval
PrimeStack compliance team verifies the bank details against AML/KYB records. Approval typically takes 1–2 business days.
4
Test Settlement
A micro-deposit of $0.01–$1.00 is sent to confirm the account is active and details are correct. Confirm receipt within 24 hours.
5
Go Live
Bank is activated on the PrimeStack network. All future ISO 20022 payment instructions will auto-populate your creditor fields.

04 — ISO 20022 Auto-Population
Once registered, PrimeStack maps your bank details to the following ISO 20022 creditor fields automatically in every pain.001 (Customer Credit Transfer) and pacs.008 (FI Credit Transfer) message:

CdtrAgt (Creditor Agent) → SWIFT/BIC
Cdtr (Creditor) → Account Holder Name + Address
CdtrAcct (Creditor Account) → IBAN or Account Number
CdtrAcct/Tp → Account type (IBAN / BBAN / ACCT)
InstrForCdtrAgt → Routing instructions if intermediary required
⚠
Important: Account holder name must match exactly with the name registered at your bank. Mismatches will cause payment rejections under ISO 20022 creditor verification rules.
