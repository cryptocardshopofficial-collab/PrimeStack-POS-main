Step-by-step integration

Follow these steps in order, and use only the environment and capabilities approved for your account.

    Authentication

    Two schemes are accepted, depending on the route family.

    SchemeScopeHow
    HMAC Merchant & agent routes Send x-primestack-key-id, x-primestack-timestamp (ms), x-primestack-nonce, and x-primestack-signature = HMAC-SHA256 of keyId + timestamp + nonce + body. Timestamps must be within 5 minutes and every nonce is single-use.
    JWT Operator /admin/* and network-management routes Obtain a token from POST /api/admin/login and replay it as Authorization: Bearer <token>.
